A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty. A skeleton who waited 13 years for GTA VI is finally playing it on the couch, and make.fun's ghost sits beside him holding its magenta star coin. The post itself is the entry: the meme is posted on X from @arshiaranjbar2x, and POST.json names the post.

## The meme

meme.png — "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR THIS. WORTH IT." A dusty skeleton, controller in hand, in front of a TV showing a neon Vice City sunset; the make.fun ghost sits on the couch arm next to him. The picture was generated from the make.fun press kit's ghost mark, and the caption was set in Anton so it stays sharp at 480 by 480.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

main.ts copies POST.json to the output directory, where the house reads the post's address and fetches the post from X.
